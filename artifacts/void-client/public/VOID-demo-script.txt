VOID — ONE-MINUTE DEMO
Caption Script & Shot List
0:00–0:05
Title card. VOID logo on dark concrete. Chiptune blip.

CAPTION:
VOID
Private video rooms.
No accounts.

0:05–0:12
Screen recording. StartScreen. Host clicks NEW SESSION. Lightning invoice appears. Payment completes. Bleep.

CAPTION:
The host pays 1,000 sats.
Approximately one cent.
Enough to mean it.

0:12–0:18
The six-word VOID Phrase appears on screen. Host copies the link. Show the phrase clearly.

CAPTION:
Six words appear.
These words are the room.
Share them with someone you trust.

0:18–0:25
Split screen. Guest opens the link on a different device. No signup screen. No download prompt. They are in the room. Bloop on connect.

CAPTION:
The guest clicks the link.
No account required.
No account possible.
They are in.

0:25–0:40
Split screen. Left: Clear mode — normal webcam. Right: Gold → Pixel → Contour → Silhouette → ASCII, cycling slowly. Then a voice mask toggle — the voice changes audibly.

CAPTION (on Clear side):
This is what most tools send.
Your face. In full.
Stored somewhere.
Forever.

CAPTION (on masked side):
This is what VOID sends.

Beat. Let the filters cycle.

CAPTION:
Enough presence to trust.
Not enough to surveil.

Voice mask toggles. The audio shifts.

CAPTION:
Your voice, too.
Processed locally.
Before it leaves your device.

0:40–0:48
Two people talking through the filters. Natural. Unhurried. Body language visible. A laugh, maybe.

CAPTION:
This is a real conversation.
Between two real people.
Who would prefer to remain that way.

0:48–0:55
Host leaves. The room disappears. Back to StartScreen. Clean. Empty. Nothing.

CAPTION:
The host leaves.
The room is gone.
Nothing was saved.
Nothing was logged.
Nothing remains.

Beat.

CAPTION:
This was intentional.

0:55–1:00
Closing card. Dark concrete. Gold text. Silence.

CAPTION:
Meet in real time.
Leave less behind.

URL.

1,000 SATS PER SESSION.
NO ACCOUNT.
LIGHTNING ONLY.

Silence.

PRODUCTION NOTES — UNCHANGED

No narration. Captions only, in the mono/gold UI style. Chiptune sound effects from the app itself — the bleep on payment, the bloop on connect, the click on share. Screen recording at 320×240 native resolution, upscaled. The brutalist UI is the production design. The product is its own set.

A NOTE ON THE SILENCE AT THE END

Vonnegut ended things abruptly when he had said what needed saying.

The room is gone. There is nothing left to show. Holding on the closing card for five seconds of silence is not a pacing choice. It is the point of the whole video, expressed without words.

Let it breathe.
