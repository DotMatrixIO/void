**Status:** retired. The video no longer plays a voiceover — the in-page player
(`VideoWithControls.tsx`) now plays a background music bed only
(`public/audio/music.mp3`). This file is kept for the on-screen "do-not-duplicate"
note below and as a record of the original narration direction. If a voiceover is
ever reintroduced, keep roughly the same total length so the scene timeline stays

**Total runtime:** ~29.3s (intro 3.0 · scan 8.0 · caption1 4.5 · caption2 4.5 ·
endCard 9.3).

**Voice direction:** calm, low register, unhurried, almost no inflection — a
quiet observation, not a sell. Second person. The voiceover carries the
emotional arc; the on-screen text carries the specifics, so the two never say
the same words. Leave space on the closing so "leave less behind" and the
wordmark land on their own.
